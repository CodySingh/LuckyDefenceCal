How levels work

  • ★ Lv 6 and Lv 12 are each unit's own milestones. Many unlock a new skill, which is usually the biggest power jump.
  • ▲ Other Lv 6 and Lv 12 milestones upgrade an existing skill.
  • + Every unit gets the same boosts at Lv 3 (+10% attack), Lv 9 (+10% attack speed), Lv 10 (treasure slot) and Lv 15 (+10% attack and attack speed).
