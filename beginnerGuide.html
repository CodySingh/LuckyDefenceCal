Level goals: 6, 12 and 15

Not yet verified for 2026

Mythics get a big power jump at Lv 6 and Lv 12, so most players stop at one of those before moving to the next unit.

  • Lv 1 → 6: 115 stones, 23,000 gold
  • Lv 6 → 12: 940 stones, 188,000 gold
  • Lv 12 → 15: 1,100 stones, 220,000 gold

The costs are confirmed for 2.0.14. The advice to stop at Lv 6 and Lv 12 comes from older fan guides.

Unlocking Immortals

Verified 2026
  • Getting a Mythic to Lv 15 lets you unlock its Immortal form for 6,000 gems.
  • Immortals level up with Immortal Stones (100 gems each). Unlike Mythic Stones, they don't drop from Recruit.
  • Since the 2nd-anniversary update, Immortals go up to Lv 25. Lv 16–25 use Breakthrough Stones and coins, each attempt can fail, and the unit gets stronger at Lv 16, 19, 22 and 25.

Source: namu.wiki Immortal page, edited Oct 11, 2026. See every Immortal on the Units page.

Older details

Not yet verified for 2026
  • The Immortal tab opens once any Mythic reaches Lv 12.
  • Immortal Stones come from God Mode, the Guild shop, the PvP shop, the normal shop and events.
  • Immortal levels 1–15 cost the same stones as Mythic levels but twice the gold.

Source: Lucky Defense Guides (legacy mirror), undated.

Leveling order (early 2026)

A community tier list from early 2026 suggests this order. Newer units such as Fairy Queen Emily aren't in it.

  1. Mythic Lv 6: Hailey, Bat Man, Yang Mage, Lancelot, Uchi, Rocket Chu, Ato, Monopoly
  2. Mythic Lv 12: Hailey, Uchi, Rocket Chu, Bat Man, Yang Mage, Lancelot
  3. Immortal Lv 6: Hailey, Bat Man, Ato, plus one of Mama, Bane or Block
  4. Immortal Lv 11–12: Bat Man, Bane, Loca, Block (to Lv 12)
  5. Immortal Lv 15: Bane, Loca, Bat Man, Block

Source: Vortex Gaming, “Mythic & Immortal Tier List Update (Including Coldie) and Leveling Order Guide”.
